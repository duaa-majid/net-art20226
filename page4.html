The Day Everything Went Wrong

Page 4: Catch the Bus

I made it onto the bus just before the doors closed.

I sat down and finally had a chance to breathe.

My stomach was still growling because I had skipped breakfast.

I looked through my backpack for something to eat.

Nothing.

I guess I would just have to survive until lunch.

After what felt like forever, the bus finally arrived at school.
