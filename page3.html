The Day Everything Went Wrong

Page 3: Eat Breakfast

I decided that being hungry all morning would be worse than missing the bus.

I quickly made some toast and ate it.

It only took a few minutes, but when I looked outside, I saw the bus driving away.

"No way."

I grabbed my backpack and ran outside anyway, but it was already too late.

I was going to have to find another way to school.
