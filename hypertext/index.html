Page 1: Waking Up Late

My alarm was supposed to go off at 7:00 AM.

Instead, I woke up at 7:20 AM.

I immediately looked at the clock and panicked. I had school in less than an hour, and I still had to get ready.

I quickly got dressed and grabbed my backpack.

Then I realized I had one more problem: I hadn't eaten breakfast.

My bus would be here soon. I could either skip breakfast and try to catch the bus, or eat something and risk missing it.
