The Day Everything Went Wrong

Page 5: Miss the Bus

I watched the bus disappear down the street.

I couldn't believe I had actually missed it.

I called my mom and explained what happened.

Luckily, she was able to drive me to school.

It took longer than the bus would have, but I eventually made it.

At least I had eaten breakfast.

I walked into school and headed to class.
