The Day Everything Went Wrong

Page 2: Skip Breakfast

I looked at the clock again.

There was no way I had time to eat.

I grabbed my backpack and ran out the door.

My stomach was already growling, but I ignored it.

I could see the bus at the end of the street.

I ran as fast as I could.

Luckily, the bus driver saw me and waited.

I made it!
