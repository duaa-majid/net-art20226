The Day Everything Went Wrong

Page 11: Reflection

By the time I got home, I was exhausted.

I thought about everything that had happened that day.

Waking up late had started the whole mess, but all of the little choices I made afterward changed how the day turned out.

If I had skipped breakfast, I caught the bus but spent the morning hungry.

If I ate breakfast, I missed the bus but at least I wasn't hungry.

And when I forgot my homework, being honest meant losing some points, while lying meant getting detention and a zero.

Maybe tomorrow I should just set more alarms.

"Tomorrow I set 3 alarms..."
